Online Quran Classes

Learn the Quran online, one-to-one, with a qualified teacher — for children and adults, from anywhere in the world.

Noorani Qaida

For beginners: the Arabic alphabet, joining letters, and the first steps of reading.

Nazra (Quran Reading)

Fluent reading of the Holy Quran with correct pronunciation.

Tajweed

The rules of beautiful, correct recitation, with practice and correction.

Hifz (Memorisation)

A structured plan to memorise the Quran, with daily revision and a teacher's guidance.

Learning prayer, its duas and its meaning — for children and new Muslims.

Islamic Studies

Basic beliefs (Aqaid), daily duas and Islamic manners for children.

How to enrol

Classes are held online at a time that suits you. To enrol, or to book a free trial lesson, please use the contact page and tell us the student's age, level and preferred timings. Fees and timings will be confirmed by the site owner before launch.
