Salaat — How to Pray

Salaat is one of the five pillars of Islam. This guide explains Wudu, the rak'ahs of each prayer and the steps of prayer, in plain words.

What is Salaat?

Salaat is the direct connection between a Muslim and Allah, performed five times a day: Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha. The Prophet ﷺ called it the coolness of his eyes.

How to perform Wudu (Ablution), step by step

  1. Make the intention and say Bismillah.
  2. Wash both hands three times, up to the wrists.
  3. Rinse the mouth three times and clean the nose three times.
  4. Wash the face three times, from forehead to chin and ear to ear.
  5. Wash the right forearm, then the left, up to and including the elbows, three times each.
  6. Wipe the head once and clean the ears.
  7. Wash the right foot, then the left, up to the ankles, three times each.

How to perform Salaat, step by step

  1. Stand facing the Qibla, make the intention, and say "Allahu Akbar" (Takbir) raising both hands.
  2. Recite Sana, then Surah Al-Fatiha, then another Surah or verses.
  3. Bow (Ruku) saying "Subhana Rabbiyal Azim" three times.
  4. Stand up saying "Sami'allahu liman hamidah, Rabbana lakal hamd."
  5. Prostrate (Sajdah) saying "Subhana Rabbiyal A'la" three times, then sit, then prostrate again.
  6. Stand for the next rak'ah and repeat. In the second rak'ah, sit for Tashahhud after the second Sajdah.
  7. In the final sitting, recite Tashahhud, Durood-e-Ibrahimi and duas, then end with Salam to the right and to the left.

Common mistakes in Salaat

  • Rushing through the movements without pausing in each position.
  • Not reciting Surah Al-Fatiha in every rak'ah.
  • Looking around or letting the mind wander — try to understand the words you recite.
  • Praying without Wudu, or with clothes that do not cover properly.
How many times a day do Muslims pray?

Five times: Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha.

What if I miss a prayer?

Pray it as soon as you remember, with sincere repentance, and try not to delay prayers in future.
