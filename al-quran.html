Understanding the Holy Quran

The Quran has 114 Surahs and 30 Paras (Juz). Surahs revealed in Makkah are called Makki and those revealed in Madinah are called Madani. Read it with Tajweed, slowly, and with its meaning — a little each day is better than a lot in a hurry.
