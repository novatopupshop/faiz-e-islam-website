Durood Sharif

Sending Durood upon the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ brings blessings, peace of heart and closeness to Allah. Below are the most read Duroods.

Durood-e-Ibrahimi

The Durood recited in every prayer, after Tashahhud.

اَللّٰهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلٰى مُحَمَّدٍ وَّعَلٰى اٰلِ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلٰى اِبْرَاهِيْمَ وَعَلٰى اٰلِ اِبْرَاهِيْمَ اِنَّكَ حَمِيْدٌ مَّجِيْدٌ
اَللّٰهُمَّ بَارِكْ عَلٰى مُحَمَّدٍ وَّعَلٰى اٰلِ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا بَارَكْتَ عَلٰى اِبْرَاهِيْمَ وَعَلٰى اٰلِ اِبْرَاهِيْمَ اِنَّكَ حَمِيْدٌ مَّجِيْدٌ

Transliteration: Allahumma salli ala Muhammadin wa ala aali Muhammadin kama sallayta ala Ibrahima wa ala aali Ibrahima innaka Hamidum Majid. Allahumma barik ala Muhammadin wa ala aali Muhammadin kama barakta ala Ibrahima wa ala aali Ibrahima innaka Hamidum Majid.

English: O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent blessings upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. Indeed, You are Praiseworthy, Glorious. O Allah, bless Muhammad and the family of Muhammad, as You blessed Ibrahim and the family of Ibrahim. Indeed, You are Praiseworthy, Glorious.

Durood-e-Nariya

A well-known Durood recited for relief from difficulties and for the fulfilment of needs. It is traditionally recited 11 times or 41 times with a sincere heart, after which dua is made.

اَللّٰهُمَّ صَلِّ صَلَاةً كَامِلَةً وَّسَلِّمْ سَلَامًا تَامًّا عَلٰى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍنِ الَّذِيْ تَنْحَلُّ بِهِ الْعُقَدُ وَتَنْفَرِجُ بِهِ الْكُرَبُ

Durood-e-Tanjeena

Known as the Durood of deliverance, recited in times of hardship and fear, seeking Allah's protection through the blessings of the Prophet ﷺ.

اَللّٰهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلٰى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ صَلَاةً تُنْجِيْنَا بِهَا مِنْ جَمِيْعِ الْاَهْوَالِ وَالْاٰفَاتِ

Durood-e-Mahi

A short Durood, easy to memorise and recite in large numbers after prayers and in daily dhikr.

اَللّٰهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلٰى مُحَمَّدٍ وَّاٰلِهٖ وَصَحْبِهٖ وَبَارِكْ وَسَلِّمْ

Why recite Durood?

The Quran commands the believers: "Indeed, Allah and His angels send blessings upon the Prophet. O you who believe, send blessings upon him and greet him with peace." (Quran 33:56). The Prophet ﷺ said that whoever sends one blessing upon him, Allah sends ten blessings in return.
