Hajj 2026 — Complete Guide, Dates and Daily Rituals

Hajj is the fifth pillar of Islam and a journey every Muslim dreams of making once in a lifetime. This guide explains, in plain words, what Hajj is, when it takes place in 2026, and what happens on each day from the 8th to the 13th of Dhul-Hijjah.

When is Hajj 2026?

Hajj falls in the Islamic month of Dhul-Hijjah. The main days run from the 8th to the 13th of Dhul-Hijjah. The exact Gregorian dates depend on the sighting of the moon, so pilgrims should confirm the final dates with their Hajj group and the Saudi authorities closer to the time.

The five days, step by step

8 Dhul-Hijjah — Day of Tarwiyah: Pilgrims enter Ihram, make the intention for Hajj, and travel to Mina, where they pray Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha and spend the night.

9 Dhul-Hijjah — Day of Arafah: The most important day of Hajj. Pilgrims gather on the plain of Arafah from noon until sunset, praying and asking Allah for forgiveness. The Prophet ﷺ said Hajj is Arafah.

10 Dhul-Hijjah — Eid day: After Fajr in Muzdalifah, pilgrims go to Mina, stone the largest pillar (Jamarat al-Aqaba), offer the sacrifice, shave or trim the hair, and perform Tawaf al-Ifadah in Makkah.

11–13 Dhul-Hijjah — Days of Tashreeq: Pilgrims stay in Mina and stone all three pillars each day, remembering Allah and making dua.

How to prepare

Learn the rituals before you travel, keep your documents and health requirements ready, pack light, and go with patience — Hajj is a test of the body and the heart together. Above all, go with a sincere intention: Hajj is for Allah alone.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hajj obligatory? Yes, once in a lifetime, for every Muslim who is physically and financially able.

Can women perform Hajj alone? Rules on mahram and group travel change from time to time; check the current rules with your Hajj operator before booking.
