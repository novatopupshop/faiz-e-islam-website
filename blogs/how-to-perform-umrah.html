How to Perform Umrah — A Complete Step-by-Step Guide

Umrah is the lesser pilgrimage to Makkah. Unlike Hajj, it can be performed at any time of the year. This guide walks through it in simple steps.

Before you travel

Learn the steps, arrange your visa and travel, and make a sincere intention. Pack Ihram clothes (for men, two plain white cloths), comfortable shoes, and a small bag for your valuables.

The four steps of Umrah

1. Ihram: At the Miqat, bathe, wear Ihram, pray two rak'ahs and make the intention: "Labbayk Allahumma Umrah." Then recite the Talbiyah.

2. Tawaf: In the Haram, circle the Kaaba seven times, starting at the Black Stone, praying as you walk.

3. Sa'i: Walk seven times between Safa and Marwah, remembering the search of Hajar (A.S.) for water for her son Ismail (A.S.).

4. Halq or Taqsir: Shave the head or trim the hair. With that, Umrah is complete and Ihram ends.

After Umrah

Pray in the Haram as much as you can, visit the Prophet's Mosque in Madinah if you are able, and carry the calm of the journey home with you — Umrah is meant to change the heart, not just the passport.
