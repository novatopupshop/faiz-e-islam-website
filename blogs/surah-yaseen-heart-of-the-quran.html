Surah Yaseen — The Heart of the Quran: Benefits and Overview

Surah Yaseen is the 36th Surah of the Holy Quran. It has 83 verses and was revealed in Makkah. Muslims know it well — it is read in the morning, at times of difficulty, and beside the sick — and it is often called the Heart of the Quran.

What is Surah Yaseen about?

The Surah begins with an oath by the wise Quran and the messengership of the Prophet ﷺ. It tells the story of a town that rejected its messengers, and of one man who ran from the far side of the city to support them. Then it turns to the signs of Allah in creation — the dead earth brought to life by rain, the night and the day, the sun and the moon in their orbits — and ends with the power of Allah to bring the dead back to life.

Why do people read it every day?

Many Muslims read Yaseen in the morning as a way to start the day with the Quran. Its verses remind us that life is short, that Allah's signs are all around us, and that the return to Him is real. Reading it slowly, with its meaning, is better than rushing through it.

How to read it

Read it with wudu, in a quiet place, verse by verse. After finishing, make dua for yourself, your family and those who have passed away. You can read it on our Surah Yaseen page with Arabic, Urdu and English translation and audio.
