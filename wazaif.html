Wazaif

Wazaif are regular recitations from the Quran and the Sunnah, done with a sincere intention for a need — for livelihood, health, protection, marriage and peace of heart. A wazifa is not magic: it is dua through the words of Allah, with patience and trust.

Wazifa for Rizq (Livelihood)

Recite Surah Al-Waqiah once after Maghrib every evening, and make dua for halal provision. Keep the recitation regular and give charity, even a little, every week.

Wazifa for Shifa (Healing)

Recite Surah Al-Fatiha seven times, blow gently on water, and drink it — and continue the treatment your doctor has given you. Healing is from Allah, and medicine is one of His means.

Wazifa for Protection

Recite Ayat-ul-Kursi (Quran 2:255) after every prayer, and Surah Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq and An-Nas three times in the morning and evening.

Wazifa for Marriage

Perform Istikhara first, consult your family, and recite Durood Sharif 100 times daily with the intention of a good, righteous spouse. Haste in marriage often brings regret; patience and counsel are part of the answer.

Wazifa for Peace of Heart

Recite "La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah" 100 times a day, and Surah Ar-Rahman with its meaning once a week. The Quran says: "Verily, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest." (13:28)

Wazifa for Forgiveness

Recite Astaghfirullah 100 times after Fajr and after Isha, and Sayyid-ul-Istighfar once in the morning and once in the evening. See the Daily Duas page for the full text.

A wazifa is done with the belief that benefit comes only from Allah. Avoid any practice that asks for money, promises guaranteed results, or adds words that are not from the Quran or authentic Sunnah.
