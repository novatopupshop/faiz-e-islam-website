About Faiz e Islam

Faiz e Islam is a free Islamic learning platform. We aim to make the Quran, Durood, Duas, Kalimas and Salaat easy to read, listen to and understand — with Urdu and English translation, audio recitation and clear guides.

What we offer

  • The complete Holy Quran — all 114 Surahs with translation and audio.
  • Durood, the six Kalimas, and more than 20 daily duas.
  • Step-by-step guides to Salaat and Istikhara.
  • Islamic blogs, Naat lyrics and Wazaif.
  • Online Quran classes for children and adults.

Our promise

All resources on this site are free, with no subscription and no paywall. Running the platform has costs — hosting, domain and the time of our team — so donations are welcome, but they are never required to read or listen.

Quran text and translations on this site are provided by AlQuran.cloud, and audio by the Islamic Network CDN. The Arabic of the Quran and the text of hadith are kept as they are; our own introductions, guides and answers are written freshly for this site.
