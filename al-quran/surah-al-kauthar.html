Surah Al-Kawthar — Abundance

Surah 108 of 1143 AyahsMakkiPara 30Also known as: Al-Kawthar

Surah Al-Kawthar is the 108th Surah of the Holy Quran. It was revealed in Makkah, before the Hijrah, which makes it a Makki Surah, and it has 3 verses. Its name means “Abundance”. It is one of the short Surahs of the Quran, often among the first that children memorise. On this page you can read it verse by verse with Urdu and English translation, listen to the recitation by Mishary Rashid Alafasy, and reflect on its message. The Surah begins in Para 30.

Full text — Arabic, Urdu, English (verse by verse)

Ayah 1

بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِیمِ إِنَّاۤ أَعۡطَیۡنَـٰكَ ٱلۡكَوۡثَرَ

یقیناً ہم نے تجھے (حوض) کوﺛر (اور بہت کچھ) دیا ہے

English: Indeed, We have granted you, [O Muhammad], al-Kawthar.

Ayah 2

فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَٱنۡحَرۡ

پس تو اپنے رب کے لئے نماز پڑھ اور قربانی کر

English: So pray to your Lord and sacrifice [to Him alone].

Ayah 3

إِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ ٱلۡأَبۡتَرُ

یقیناً تیرا دشمن ہی ﻻوارث اور بے نام ونشان ہے

English: Indeed, your enemy is the one cut off.

Virtues and benefits

  • Reading Surah Al-Kawthar is reading the words of Allah — every letter brings reward, and reading with its meaning brings understanding with it.
  • Make it part of your daily routine: a few verses each day, with their Urdu or English translation, is better than rushing through many pages without understanding.
  • Teach it to your children and share it with your family — the Quran is meant to be read, understood and lived by, not only kept on a shelf.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many verses are in Surah Al-Kawthar?

Surah Al-Kawthar has 3 verses.

Is Surah Al-Kawthar Makki or Madani?

Makki — it was revealed in Makkah.

What does the name Al-Kawthar mean?

It means “Abundance”.

Which para (Juz) does Surah Al-Kawthar begin in?

Para 30.
