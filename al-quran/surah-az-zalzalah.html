Surah Az-Zalzala — The Earthquake

Surah 99 of 1148 AyahsMadaniPara 30Also known as: Az-Zalzala

Surah Az-Zalzala is the 99th Surah of the Holy Quran. It was revealed in Madinah, after the Hijrah, which makes it a Madani Surah, and it has 8 verses. Its name means “The Earthquake”. It is one of the short Surahs of the Quran, often among the first that children memorise. On this page you can read it verse by verse with Urdu and English translation, listen to the recitation by Mishary Rashid Alafasy, and reflect on its message. The Surah begins in Para 30.

Full text — Arabic, Urdu, English (verse by verse)

Ayah 1

بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِیمِ إِذَا زُلۡزِلَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ زِلۡزَالَهَا

جب زمین پوری طرح جھنجھوڑ دی جائے گی

English: When the earth is shaken with its [final] earthquake

Ayah 2

وَأَخۡرَجَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ أَثۡقَالَهَا

اور اپنے بوجھ باہر نکال پھینکے گی

English: And the earth discharges its burdens

Ayah 3

وَقَالَ ٱلۡإِنسَـٰنُ مَا لَهَا

انسان کہنے لگے گا کہ اسے کیا ہوگیا؟

English: And man says, "What is [wrong] with it?" -

Ayah 4

یَوۡمَىِٕذࣲ تُحَدِّثُ أَخۡبَارَهَا

اس دن زمین اپنی سب خبریں بیان کردے گی

English: That Day, it will report its news

Ayah 5

بِأَنَّ رَبَّكَ أَوۡحَىٰ لَهَا

اس لئے کہ تیرے رب نے اسے حکم دیا ہوگا

English: Because your Lord has commanded it.

Ayah 6

یَوۡمَىِٕذࣲ یَصۡدُرُ ٱلنَّاسُ أَشۡتَاتࣰا لِّیُرَوۡا۟ أَعۡمَـٰلَهُمۡ

اس روز لوگ مختلف جماعتیں ہو کر (واپس) لوٹیں گے تاکہ انہیں ان کے اعمال دکھا دیئے جائیں

English: That Day, the people will depart separated [into categories] to be shown [the result of] their deeds.

Ayah 7

فَمَن یَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَیۡرࣰا یَرَهُۥ

پس جس نے ذره برابر نیکی کی ہوگی وه اسے دیکھ لے گا

English: So whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it,

Ayah 8

وَمَن یَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةࣲ شَرࣰّا یَرَهُۥ

اور جس نے ذره برابر برائی کی ہوگی وه اسے دیکھ لے گا

English: And whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it.

Virtues and benefits

  • Reading Surah Az-Zalzala is reading the words of Allah — every letter brings reward, and reading with its meaning brings understanding with it.
  • Make it part of your daily routine: a few verses each day, with their Urdu or English translation, is better than rushing through many pages without understanding.
  • Teach it to your children and share it with your family — the Quran is meant to be read, understood and lived by, not only kept on a shelf.
