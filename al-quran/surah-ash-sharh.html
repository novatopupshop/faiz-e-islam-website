Surah Ash-Sharh — The Consolation

Surah 94 of 1148 AyahsMakkiPara 30Also known as: Ash-Sharh

Surah Ash-Sharh is the 94th Surah of the Holy Quran. It was revealed in Makkah, before the Hijrah, which makes it a Makki Surah, and it has 8 verses. Its name means “The Consolation”. It is one of the short Surahs of the Quran, often among the first that children memorise. On this page you can read it verse by verse with Urdu and English translation, listen to the recitation by Mishary Rashid Alafasy, and reflect on its message. The Surah begins in Para 30.

Full text — Arabic, Urdu, English (verse by verse)

Ayah 1

بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِیمِ أَلَمۡ نَشۡرَحۡ لَكَ صَدۡرَكَ

کیا ہم نے تیرا سینہ نہیں کھول دیا

English: Did We not expand for you, [O Muhammad], your breast?

Ayah 2

وَوَضَعۡنَا عَنكَ وِزۡرَكَ

اور تجھ پر سے تیرا بوجھ ہم نے اتار دیا

English: And We removed from you your burden

Ayah 3

ٱلَّذِیۤ أَنقَضَ ظَهۡرَكَ

جس نے تیری پیٹھ توڑ دی تھی

English: Which had weighed upon your back

Ayah 4

وَرَفَعۡنَا لَكَ ذِكۡرَكَ

اور ہمنے تیرا ذکر بلند کر دیا

English: And raised high for you your repute.

Ayah 5

فَإِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ یُسۡرًا

پس یقیناً مشکل کے ساتھ آسانی ہے

English: For indeed, with hardship [will be] ease.

Ayah 6

إِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ یُسۡرࣰا

بیشک مشکل کے ساتھ آسانی ہے

English: Indeed, with hardship [will be] ease.

Ayah 7

فَإِذَا فَرَغۡتَ فَٱنصَبۡ

پس جب تو فارغ ہو تو عبادت میں محنت کر

English: So when you have finished [your duties], then stand up [for worship].

Ayah 8

وَإِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَٱرۡغَب

اور اپنے پروردگار ہی کی طرف دل لگا

English: And to your Lord direct [your] longing.

Virtues and benefits

  • Reading Surah Ash-Sharh is reading the words of Allah — every letter brings reward, and reading with its meaning brings understanding with it.
  • Make it part of your daily routine: a few verses each day, with their Urdu or English translation, is better than rushing through many pages without understanding.
  • Teach it to your children and share it with your family — the Quran is meant to be read, understood and lived by, not only kept on a shelf.
