Surah Al-Aadiyaat — The Chargers

Surah 100 of 11411 AyahsMakkiPara 30Also known as: Al-Aadiyaat

Surah Al-Aadiyaat is the 100th Surah of the Holy Quran. It was revealed in Makkah, before the Hijrah, which makes it a Makki Surah, and it has 11 verses. Its name means “The Chargers”. It is a Surah of moderate length, easy to read in one sitting. On this page you can read it verse by verse with Urdu and English translation, listen to the recitation by Mishary Rashid Alafasy, and reflect on its message. The Surah begins in Para 30.

Full text — Arabic, Urdu, English (verse by verse)

Ayah 1

بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِیمِ وَٱلۡعَـٰدِیَـٰتِ ضَبۡحࣰا

ہانپتے ہوئے دوڑنے والے گھوڑوں کی قسم!

English: By the racers, panting,

Ayah 2

فَٱلۡمُورِیَـٰتِ قَدۡحࣰا

پھر ٹاپ مار کر آگ جھاڑنے والوں کی قسم!

English: And the producers of sparks [when] striking

Ayah 3

فَٱلۡمُغِیرَ ٰ⁠تِ صُبۡحࣰا

پھر صبح کے وقت دھاوا بولنے والوں کی قسم

English: And the chargers at dawn,

Ayah 4

فَأَثَرۡنَ بِهِۦ نَقۡعࣰا

پس اس وقت گرد وغبار اڑاتے ہیں

English: Stirring up thereby [clouds of] dust,

Ayah 5

فَوَسَطۡنَ بِهِۦ جَمۡعًا

پھر اسی کے ساتھ فوجیوں کے درمیان گھس جاتے ہیں

English: Arriving thereby in the center collectively,

Ayah 6

إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَـٰنَ لِرَبِّهِۦ لَكَنُودࣱ

یقیناً انسان اپنے رب کا بڑا ناشکرا ہے

English: Indeed mankind, to his Lord, is ungrateful.

Ayah 7

وَإِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ ذَ ٰ⁠لِكَ لَشَهِیدࣱ

اور یقیناً وه خود بھی اس پر گواه ہے

English: And indeed, he is to that a witness.

Ayah 8

وَإِنَّهُۥ لِحُبِّ ٱلۡخَیۡرِ لَشَدِیدٌ

یہ مال کی محنت میں بھی بڑا سخت ہے

English: And indeed he is, in love of wealth, intense.

Ayah 9

۞ أَفَلَا یَعۡلَمُ إِذَا بُعۡثِرَ مَا فِی ٱلۡقُبُورِ

کیا اسے وه وقت معلوم نہیں جب قبروں میں جو (کچھ) ہے نکال لیا جائے گا

English: But does he not know that when the contents of the graves are scattered

Ayah 10

وَحُصِّلَ مَا فِی ٱلصُّدُورِ

اور سینوں کی پوشیده باتیں ﻇاہر کر دی جائیں گی

English: And that within the breasts is obtained,

Ayah 11

إِنَّ رَبَّهُم بِهِمۡ یَوۡمَىِٕذࣲ لَّخَبِیرُۢ

بیشک ان کا رب اس دن ان کے حال سے پورا باخبر ہوگا

English: Indeed, their Lord with them, that Day, is [fully] Acquainted.

Virtues and benefits

  • Reading Surah Al-Aadiyaat is reading the words of Allah — every letter brings reward, and reading with its meaning brings understanding with it.
  • Make it part of your daily routine: a few verses each day, with their Urdu or English translation, is better than rushing through many pages without understanding.
  • Teach it to your children and share it with your family — the Quran is meant to be read, understood and lived by, not only kept on a shelf.
