Surah An-Nasr — Divine Support

Surah 110 of 1143 AyahsMadaniPara 30Also known as: An-Nasr

Surah An-Nasr is the 110th Surah of the Holy Quran. It was revealed in Madinah, after the Hijrah, which makes it a Madani Surah, and it has 3 verses. Its name means “Divine Support”. It is one of the short Surahs of the Quran, often among the first that children memorise. On this page you can read it verse by verse with Urdu and English translation, listen to the recitation by Mishary Rashid Alafasy, and reflect on its message. The Surah begins in Para 30.

Full text — Arabic, Urdu, English (verse by verse)

Ayah 1

بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِیمِ إِذَا جَاۤءَ نَصۡرُ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡفَتۡحُ

جب اللہ کی مدد اور فتح آجائے

English: When the victory of Allah has come and the conquest,

Ayah 2

وَرَأَیۡتَ ٱلنَّاسَ یَدۡخُلُونَ فِی دِینِ ٱللَّهِ أَفۡوَاجࣰا

اور تو لوگوں کو اللہ کے دین میں جوق در جوق آتا دیکھ لے

English: And you see the people entering into the religion of Allah in multitudes,

Ayah 3

فَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡهُۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ تَوَّابَۢا

تو اپنے رب کی تسبیح کرنے لگ حمد کے ساتھ اور اس سے مغفرت کی دعا مانگ، بیشک وه بڑا ہی توبہ قبول کرنے واﻻ ہے

English: Then exalt [Him] with praise of your Lord and ask forgiveness of Him. Indeed, He is ever Accepting of repentance.

Virtues and benefits

  • Reading Surah An-Nasr is reading the words of Allah — every letter brings reward, and reading with its meaning brings understanding with it.
  • Make it part of your daily routine: a few verses each day, with their Urdu or English translation, is better than rushing through many pages without understanding.
  • Teach it to your children and share it with your family — the Quran is meant to be read, understood and lived by, not only kept on a shelf.
