Surah Al-Humaza — The Traducer

Surah 104 of 1149 AyahsMakkiPara 30Also known as: Al-Humaza

Surah Al-Humaza is the 104th Surah of the Holy Quran. It was revealed in Makkah, before the Hijrah, which makes it a Makki Surah, and it has 9 verses. Its name means “The Traducer”. It is one of the short Surahs of the Quran, often among the first that children memorise. On this page you can read it verse by verse with Urdu and English translation, listen to the recitation by Mishary Rashid Alafasy, and reflect on its message. The Surah begins in Para 30.

Full text — Arabic, Urdu, English (verse by verse)

Ayah 1

بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِیمِ وَیۡلࣱ لِّكُلِّ هُمَزَةࣲ لُّمَزَةٍ

بڑی خرابی ہے ہر ایسے شخص کی جو عیب ٹٹولنے واﻻ غیبت کرنے واﻻ ہو

English: Woe to every scorner and mocker

Ayah 2

ٱلَّذِی جَمَعَ مَالࣰا وَعَدَّدَهُۥ

جو مال کو جمع کرتا جائے اور گنتا جائے

English: Who collects wealth and [continuously] counts it.

Ayah 3

یَحۡسَبُ أَنَّ مَالَهُۥۤ أَخۡلَدَهُۥ

وه سمجھتا ہے کہ اس کا مال اس کے پاس سدا رہے گا

English: He thinks that his wealth will make him immortal.

Ayah 4

كَلَّاۖ لَیُنۢبَذَنَّ فِی ٱلۡحُطَمَةِ

ہرگز نہیں یہ تو ضرور توڑ پھوڑ دینے والی آگ میں پھینک دیا جائے گا

English: No! He will surely be thrown into the Crusher.

Ayah 5

وَمَاۤ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡحُطَمَةُ

اور تجھے کیا معلوم کہ ایسی آگ کیا ہوگی؟

English: And what can make you know what is the Crusher?

Ayah 6

نَارُ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمُوقَدَةُ

وه اللہ تعالیٰ کی سلگائی ہوئی آگ ہوگی

English: It is the fire of Allah, [eternally] fueled,

Ayah 7

ٱلَّتِی تَطَّلِعُ عَلَى ٱلۡأَفۡـِٔدَةِ

جو دلوں پر چڑھتی چلی جائے گی

English: Which mounts directed at the hearts.

Ayah 8

إِنَّهَا عَلَیۡهِم مُّؤۡصَدَةࣱ

وہ ان پر ہر طرف سے بند کی ہوئی ہوگی

English: Indeed, Hellfire will be closed down upon them

Ayah 9

فِی عَمَدࣲ مُّمَدَّدَةِۭ

بڑے بڑے ستونوں میں

English: In extended columns.

Virtues and benefits

  • Reading Surah Al-Humaza is reading the words of Allah — every letter brings reward, and reading with its meaning brings understanding with it.
  • Make it part of your daily routine: a few verses each day, with their Urdu or English translation, is better than rushing through many pages without understanding.
  • Teach it to your children and share it with your family — the Quran is meant to be read, understood and lived by, not only kept on a shelf.
