Surah At-Tin — The Fig

Surah 95 of 1148 AyahsMakkiPara 30Also known as: At-Tin

Surah At-Tin is the 95th Surah of the Holy Quran. It was revealed in Makkah, before the Hijrah, which makes it a Makki Surah, and it has 8 verses. Its name means “The Fig”. It is one of the short Surahs of the Quran, often among the first that children memorise. On this page you can read it verse by verse with Urdu and English translation, listen to the recitation by Mishary Rashid Alafasy, and reflect on its message. The Surah begins in Para 30.

Full text — Arabic, Urdu, English (verse by verse)

Ayah 1

بِّسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِیمِ وَٱلتِّینِ وَٱلزَّیۡتُونِ

قسم ہے انجیر کی اور زیتون کی

English: By the fig and the olive

Ayah 2

وَطُورِ سِینِینَ

اور طور سِینِین کی

English: And [by] Mount Sinai

Ayah 3

وَهَـٰذَا ٱلۡبَلَدِ ٱلۡأَمِینِ

اور اس امن والے شہر کی

English: And [by] this secure city [Makkah],

Ayah 4

لَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَـٰنَ فِیۤ أَحۡسَنِ تَقۡوِیمࣲ

یقیناً ہم نے انسان کو بہترین صورت میں پیدا کیا

English: We have certainly created man in the best of stature;

Ayah 5

ثُمَّ رَدَدۡنَـٰهُ أَسۡفَلَ سَـٰفِلِینَ

پھر اسے نیچوں سے نیچا کر دیا

English: Then We return him to the lowest of the low,

Ayah 6

إِلَّا ٱلَّذِینَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَـٰتِ فَلَهُمۡ أَجۡرٌ غَیۡرُ مَمۡنُونࣲ

لیکن جو لوگ ایمان ﻻئے اور (پھر) نیک عمل کیے تو ان کے لئے ایسا اجر ہے جو کبھی ختم نہ ہوگا

English: Except for those who believe and do righteous deeds, for they will have a reward uninterrupted.

Ayah 7

فَمَا یُكَذِّبُكَ بَعۡدُ بِٱلدِّینِ

پس تجھے اب روز جزا کے جھٹلانے پر کون سی چیز آماده کرتی ہے

English: So what yet causes you to deny the Recompense?

Ayah 8

أَلَیۡسَ ٱللَّهُ بِأَحۡكَمِ ٱلۡحَـٰكِمِینَ

کیا اللہ تعالیٰ (سب) حاکموں کا حاکم نہیں ہے

English: Is not Allah the most just of judges?

Virtues and benefits

  • Reading Surah At-Tin is reading the words of Allah — every letter brings reward, and reading with its meaning brings understanding with it.
  • Make it part of your daily routine: a few verses each day, with their Urdu or English translation, is better than rushing through many pages without understanding.
  • Teach it to your children and share it with your family — the Quran is meant to be read, understood and lived by, not only kept on a shelf.
