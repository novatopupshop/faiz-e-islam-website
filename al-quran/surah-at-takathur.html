Surah At-Takaathur — Competition

Surah 102 of 1148 AyahsMakkiPara 30Also known as: At-Takaathur

Surah At-Takaathur is the 102nd Surah of the Holy Quran. It was revealed in Makkah, before the Hijrah, which makes it a Makki Surah, and it has 8 verses. Its name means “Competition”. It is one of the short Surahs of the Quran, often among the first that children memorise. On this page you can read it verse by verse with Urdu and English translation, listen to the recitation by Mishary Rashid Alafasy, and reflect on its message. The Surah begins in Para 30.

Full text — Arabic, Urdu, English (verse by verse)

Ayah 1

بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِیمِ أَلۡهَىٰكُمُ ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ

زیادتی کی چاہت نے تمہیں غافل کردیا

English: Competition in [worldly] increase diverts you

Ayah 2

حَتَّىٰ زُرۡتُمُ ٱلۡمَقَابِرَ

یہاں تک کہ تم قبرستان جا پہنچے

English: Until you visit the graveyards.

Ayah 3

كَلَّا سَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ

ہرگز نہیں تم عنقریب معلوم کر لو گے

English: No! You are going to know.

Ayah 4

ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ

ہرگز نہیں پھر تمہیں جلد علم ہو جائے گا

English: Then no! You are going to know.

Ayah 5

كَلَّا لَوۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ عِلۡمَ ٱلۡیَقِینِ

ہرگز نہیں اگر تم یقینی طور پر جان لو

English: No! If you only knew with knowledge of certainty...

Ayah 6

لَتَرَوُنَّ ٱلۡجَحِیمَ

تو بیشک تم جہنم دیکھ لو گے

English: You will surely see the Hellfire.

Ayah 7

ثُمَّ لَتَرَوُنَّهَا عَیۡنَ ٱلۡیَقِینِ

اور تم اسے یقین کی آنکھ سے دیکھ لو گے

English: Then you will surely see it with the eye of certainty.

Ayah 8

ثُمَّ لَتُسۡـَٔلُنَّ یَوۡمَىِٕذٍ عَنِ ٱلنَّعِیمِ

پھر اس دن تم سے ضرور بالضرور نعمتوں کا سوال ہوگا

English: Then you will surely be asked that Day about pleasure.

Virtues and benefits

  • Reading Surah At-Takaathur is reading the words of Allah — every letter brings reward, and reading with its meaning brings understanding with it.
  • Make it part of your daily routine: a few verses each day, with their Urdu or English translation, is better than rushing through many pages without understanding.
  • Teach it to your children and share it with your family — the Quran is meant to be read, understood and lived by, not only kept on a shelf.
