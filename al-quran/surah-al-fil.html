Surah Al-Fil — The Elephant

Surah 105 of 1145 AyahsMakkiPara 30Also known as: Al-Fil

Surah Al-Fil is the 105th Surah of the Holy Quran. It was revealed in Makkah, before the Hijrah, which makes it a Makki Surah, and it has 5 verses. Its name means “The Elephant”. It is one of the short Surahs of the Quran, often among the first that children memorise. On this page you can read it verse by verse with Urdu and English translation, listen to the recitation by Mishary Rashid Alafasy, and reflect on its message. The Surah begins in Para 30.

Full text — Arabic, Urdu, English (verse by verse)

Ayah 1

بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِیمِ أَلَمۡ تَرَ كَیۡفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِأَصۡحَـٰبِ ٱلۡفِیلِ

کیا تو نے نہ دیکھا کہ تیرے رب نے ہاتھی والوں کے ساتھ کیا کیا؟

English: Have you not considered, [O Muhammad], how your Lord dealt with the companions of the elephant?

Ayah 2

أَلَمۡ یَجۡعَلۡ كَیۡدَهُمۡ فِی تَضۡلِیلࣲ

کیا ان کے مکر کو بےکار نہیں کردیا؟

English: Did He not make their plan into misguidance?

Ayah 3

وَأَرۡسَلَ عَلَیۡهِمۡ طَیۡرًا أَبَابِیلَ

اور ان پر پرندوں کے جھنڈ کے جھنڈ بھیج دیئے

English: And He sent against them birds in flocks,

Ayah 4

تَرۡمِیهِم بِحِجَارَةࣲ مِّن سِجِّیلࣲ

جو انہیں مٹی اور پتھر کی کنکریاں مار رہے تھے

English: Striking them with stones of hard clay,

Ayah 5

فَجَعَلَهُمۡ كَعَصۡفࣲ مَّأۡكُولِۭ

پس انہیں کھائے ہوئے بھوسے کی طرح کردیا

English: And He made them like eaten straw.

Virtues and benefits

  • Reading Surah Al-Fil is reading the words of Allah — every letter brings reward, and reading with its meaning brings understanding with it.
  • Make it part of your daily routine: a few verses each day, with their Urdu or English translation, is better than rushing through many pages without understanding.
  • Teach it to your children and share it with your family — the Quran is meant to be read, understood and lived by, not only kept on a shelf.
