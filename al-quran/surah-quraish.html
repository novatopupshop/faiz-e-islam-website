Surah Quraish — Quraysh

Surah 106 of 1144 AyahsMakkiPara 30Also known as: Quraish

Surah Quraish is the 106th Surah of the Holy Quran. It was revealed in Makkah, before the Hijrah, which makes it a Makki Surah, and it has 4 verses. Its name means “Quraysh”. It is one of the short Surahs of the Quran, often among the first that children memorise. On this page you can read it verse by verse with Urdu and English translation, listen to the recitation by Mishary Rashid Alafasy, and reflect on its message. The Surah begins in Para 30.

Full text — Arabic, Urdu, English (verse by verse)

Ayah 1

بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِیمِ لِإِیلَـٰفِ قُرَیۡشٍ

قریش کے مانوس کرنے کے لئے

English: For the accustomed security of the Quraysh -

Ayah 2

إِۦلَـٰفِهِمۡ رِحۡلَةَ ٱلشِّتَاۤءِ وَٱلصَّیۡفِ

(یعنی) انہیں جاڑے اور گرمی کے سفر سے مانوس کرنے کے لئے۔ (اس کے شکریہ میں)

English: Their accustomed security [in] the caravan of winter and summer -

Ayah 3

فَلۡیَعۡبُدُوا۟ رَبَّ هَـٰذَا ٱلۡبَیۡتِ

پس انہیں چاہئے کہ اسی گھر کے رب کی عبادت کرتے رہیں

English: Let them worship the Lord of this House,

Ayah 4

ٱلَّذِیۤ أَطۡعَمَهُم مِّن جُوعࣲ وَءَامَنَهُم مِّنۡ خَوۡفِۭ

جس نے انہیں بھوک میں کھانا دیا اور ڈر (اور خوف) میں امن (وامان) دیا

English: Who has fed them, [saving them] from hunger and made them safe, [saving them] from fear.

Virtues and benefits

  • Reading Surah Quraish is reading the words of Allah — every letter brings reward, and reading with its meaning brings understanding with it.
  • Make it part of your daily routine: a few verses each day, with their Urdu or English translation, is better than rushing through many pages without understanding.
  • Teach it to your children and share it with your family — the Quran is meant to be read, understood and lived by, not only kept on a shelf.

Makki — it was revealed in Makkah.
