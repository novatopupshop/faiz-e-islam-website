Surah Al-Faatiha — The Opening

Surah 1 of 1147 AyahsMakkiPara 1Also known as: Al-Faatiha

Surah Al-Fatiha is the first Surah of the Holy Quran and the one every Muslim recites in every rak'ah of every prayer. It has only seven verses, yet it holds the whole message of the Quran in brief: who Allah is, why we worship Him alone, and the one prayer every believer keeps asking — guide us to the straight path. That is why it is also called Umm al-Kitab, the Mother of the Book. On this page you can read it verse by verse with Urdu and English translation, listen to the recitation, and download it as a PDF.

Full text — Arabic, Urdu, English (verse by verse)

Ayah 1

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

شروع کرتا ہوں اللہ تعالیٰ کے نام سے جو بڑا مہربان نہایت رحم والا ہے

English: In the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful.

Ayah 2

ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ

سب تعریف اللہ تعالیٰ کے لئے ہے جو تمام جہانوں کا پالنے والا ہے

English: [All] praise is [due] to Allah, Lord of the worlds —

Ayah 3

ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

بڑا مہربان نہایت رحم کرنے والا

English: The Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful,

Ayah 4

مَـٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ

بدلے کے دن (یعنی قیامت) کا مالک ہے

English: Sovereign of the Day of Recompense.

Ayah 5

إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ

ہم صرف تیری ہی عبادت کرتے ہیں اور صرف تجھ ہی سے مدد چاہتے ہیں

English: It is You we worship and You we ask for help.

Ayah 6

ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَ ٰ⁠طَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ

ہمیں سیدھی (اور سچی) راہ دکھا

English: Guide us to the straight path —

Ayah 7

صِرَ ٰ⁠طَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّاۤلِّينَ

ان لوگوں کی راہ جن پر تو نے انعام کیا ان کی نہیں جن پر غضب کیا گیا اور نہ گمراہوں کی

English: The path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor, not of those who have evoked [Your] anger or of those who are astray.

Virtues and benefits

  • No prayer is complete without it — the Prophet ﷺ taught that the prayer of a person who does not recite Al-Fatiha is not complete, which is why it is read in every rak'ah.
  • It is a conversation with Allah: each verse is answered, so reciting it slowly, pausing after each verse, turns the prayer into a dialogue rather than a rush.
  • It is also a cure and a protection — Muslims have recited it for healing and for safety since the earliest days, and it is the first thing many parents teach their children.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many verses are in Surah Al-Fatiha?

Seven verses. It is the shortest complete Surah by count after the very short ones, and the first Surah of the Quran.

Is Surah Al-Fatiha Makki or Madani?

Makki — it was revealed in Makkah, before the Hijrah.

Can I read Surah Al-Fatiha without wudu?

You can recite it from memory without wudu, but touching the printed Quran (mushaf) requires wudu. The same rule applies on this site: reading on screen is fine.

Why is it called Umm al-Kitab?

Because it contains the essence of the whole Quran — praise of Allah, worship of Him alone, and the prayer for guidance — in just seven verses.

Which para (Juz) is Surah Al-Fatiha in?

Para 1, at the very beginning of the Quran.
