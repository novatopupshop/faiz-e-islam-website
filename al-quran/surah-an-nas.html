Surah An-Naas — Mankind

Surah 114 of 1146 AyahsMakkiPara 30Also known as: An-Naas

Surah An-Naas is the 114th Surah of the Holy Quran. It was revealed in Makkah, before the Hijrah, which makes it a Makki Surah, and it has 6 verses. Its name means “Mankind”. It is one of the short Surahs of the Quran, often among the first that children memorise. On this page you can read it verse by verse with Urdu and English translation, listen to the recitation by Mishary Rashid Alafasy, and reflect on its message. The Surah begins in Para 30.

Full text — Arabic, Urdu, English (verse by verse)

Ayah 1

بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِیمِ قُلۡ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلنَّاسِ

آپ کہہ دیجئے! کہ میں لوگوں کے پروردگار کی پناه میں آتا ہوں

English: Say, "I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind,

Ayah 2

مَلِكِ ٱلنَّاسِ

لوگوں کے مالک کی (اور)

English: The Sovereign of mankind.

Ayah 3

إِلَـٰهِ ٱلنَّاسِ

لوگوں کے معبود کی (پناه میں)

English: The God of mankind,

Ayah 4

مِن شَرِّ ٱلۡوَسۡوَاسِ ٱلۡخَنَّاسِ

وسوسہ ڈالنے والے پیچھے ہٹ جانے والے کے شر سے

English: From the evil of the retreating whisperer -

Ayah 5

ٱلَّذِی یُوَسۡوِسُ فِی صُدُورِ ٱلنَّاسِ

جو لوگوں کے سینوں میں وسوسہ ڈالتا ہے

English: Who whispers [evil] into the breasts of mankind -

Ayah 6

مِنَ ٱلۡجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ

(خواه) وه جن میں سے ہو یا انسان میں سے

English: From among the jinn and mankind."

Virtues and benefits

  • Reading Surah An-Naas is reading the words of Allah — every letter brings reward, and reading with its meaning brings understanding with it.
  • Make it part of your daily routine: a few verses each day, with their Urdu or English translation, is better than rushing through many pages without understanding.
  • Teach it to your children and share it with your family — the Quran is meant to be read, understood and lived by, not only kept on a shelf.
