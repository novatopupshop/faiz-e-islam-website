Surah Ad-Dhuhaa — The Morning Hours

Surah 93 of 11411 AyahsMakkiPara 30Also known as: Ad-Dhuhaa

Surah Ad-Dhuhaa is the 93rd Surah of the Holy Quran. It was revealed in Makkah, before the Hijrah, which makes it a Makki Surah, and it has 11 verses. Its name means “The Morning Hours”. It is a Surah of moderate length, easy to read in one sitting. On this page you can read it verse by verse with Urdu and English translation, listen to the recitation by Mishary Rashid Alafasy, and reflect on its message. The Surah begins in Para 30.

Full text — Arabic, Urdu, English (verse by verse)

Ayah 1

بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِیمِ وَٱلضُّحَىٰ

قسم ہے چاشت کے وقت کی

English: By the morning brightness

Ayah 2

وَٱلَّیۡلِ إِذَا سَجَىٰ

اور قسم ہے رات کی جب چھا جائے

English: And [by] the night when it covers with darkness,

Ayah 3

مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَىٰ

نہ تو تیرے رب نے تجھے چھوڑا ہے اور نہ وه بیزار ہو گیا ہے

English: Your Lord has not taken leave of you, [O Muhammad], nor has He detested [you].

Ayah 4

وَلَلۡـَٔاخِرَةُ خَیۡرࣱ لَّكَ مِنَ ٱلۡأُولَىٰ

یقیناً تیرے لئے انجام آغاز سے بہتر ہوگا

English: And the Hereafter is better for you than the first [life].

Ayah 5

وَلَسَوۡفَ یُعۡطِیكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرۡضَىٰۤ

تجھے تیرا رب بہت جلد (انعام) دے گا اور تو راضی (وخوش) ہو جائے گا

English: And your Lord is going to give you, and you will be satisfied.

Ayah 6

أَلَمۡ یَجِدۡكَ یَتِیمࣰا فَـَٔاوَىٰ

کیا اس نے تجھے یتیم پا کر جگہ نہیں دی

English: Did He not find you an orphan and give [you] refuge?

Ayah 7

وَوَجَدَكَ ضَاۤلࣰّا فَهَدَىٰ

اور تجھے راه بھوﻻ پا کر ہدایت نہیں دی

English: And He found you lost and guided [you],

Ayah 8

وَوَجَدَكَ عَاۤىِٕلࣰا فَأَغۡنَىٰ

اور تجھے نادار پاکر تونگر نہیں بنا دیا؟

English: And He found you poor and made [you] self-sufficient.

Ayah 9

فَأَمَّا ٱلۡیَتِیمَ فَلَا تَقۡهَرۡ

پس یتیم پر تو بھی سختی نہ کیا کر

English: So as for the orphan, do not oppress [him].

Ayah 10

وَأَمَّا ٱلسَّاۤىِٕلَ فَلَا تَنۡهَرۡ

اور نہ سوال کرنے والے کو ڈانٹ ڈپٹ

English: And as for the petitioner, do not repel [him].

Ayah 11

وَأَمَّا بِنِعۡمَةِ رَبِّكَ فَحَدِّثۡ

اور اپنے رب کی نعمتوں کو بیان کرتا ره

English: But as for the favor of your Lord, report [it].

Virtues and benefits

  • Reading Surah Ad-Dhuhaa is reading the words of Allah — every letter brings reward, and reading with its meaning brings understanding with it.
  • Make it part of your daily routine: a few verses each day, with their Urdu or English translation, is better than rushing through many pages without understanding.
  • Teach it to your children and share it with your family — the Quran is meant to be read, understood and lived by, not only kept on a shelf.
