Istikhara — The Prayer of Seeking Guidance

Istikhara is a beautiful prayer for anyone facing an important decision — marriage, a job, a move, or any choice where the right path is unclear. Through it, we ask Allah to guide us to what is good and to turn us away from what is harmful.

Dua-e-Istikhara

اَللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْتَخِيرُكَ بِعِلْمِكَ وَأَسْتَقْدِرُكَ بِقُدْرَتِكَ وَأَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ الْعَظِيمِ فَإِنَّكَ تَقْدِرُ وَلَا أَقْدِرُ وَتَعْلَمُ وَلَا أَعْلَمُ وَأَنْتَ عَلَّامُ الْغُيُوبِ اَللّٰهُمَّ إِنْ كُنْتَ تَعْلَمُ أَنَّ هَذَا الْأَمْرَ خَيْرٌ لِي فِي دِينِي وَمَعَاشِي وَعَاقِبَةِ أَمْرِي فَاقْدُرْهُ لِي وَيَسِّرْهُ لِي ثُمَّ بَارِكْ لِي فِيهِ وَإِنْ كُنْتَ تَعْلَمُ أَنَّ هَذَا الْأَمْرَ شَرٌّ لِي فِي دِينِي وَمَعَاشِي وَعَاقِبَةِ أَمْرِي فَاصْرِفْهُ عَنِّي وَاصْرِفْنِي عَنْهُ وَاقْدُرْ لِيَ الْخَيْرَ حَيْثُ كَانَ ثُمَّ أَرْضِنِي بِهِ

Transliteration: Allahumma inni astakhiruka bi ilmika, wa astaqdiruka bi qudratika, wa as'aluka min fadlikal azim, fa innaka taqdiru wa la aqdiru, wa ta'lamu wa la a'lamu, wa anta allamul ghuyub. Allahumma in kunta ta'lamu anna hadhal amra khayrun li fi dini wa ma'ashi wa aqibati amri, faqdurhu li wa yassirhu li thumma barik li fihi. Wa in kunta ta'lamu anna hadhal amra sharrun li fi dini wa ma'ashi wa aqibati amri, fasrifhu anni wasrifni anhu, waqdur liyal khayra haythu kana thumma ardini bihi.

English: O Allah, I seek Your guidance through Your knowledge, and I seek ability through Your power, and I ask You from Your great bounty. You have power and I do not, You know and I do not, and You are the Knower of the unseen. O Allah, if You know that this matter is good for me in my religion, my livelihood and the outcome of my affair, then decree it for me, make it easy for me, and bless it for me. And if You know that this matter is bad for me in my religion, my livelihood and the outcome of my affair, then turn it away from me and turn me away from it, and decree for me what is good wherever it is, then make me content with it.

How to perform Salat-ul-Istikhara, step by step

  1. Make Wudu and pray two rak'ahs of Nafl prayer (outside the obligatory prayers).
  2. After the prayer, recite the Dua-e-Istikhara above, naming your matter where the dua says "this matter".
  3. Ask with a sincere heart and then go ahead — do your research, consult trustworthy people, and take the step that seems right.
  4. Watch how matters unfold: if the path opens and your heart is at ease, that is a good sign; if doors keep closing, that too is an answer.

What Istikhara is not

Istikhara is not a dream machine. You do not have to see a dream for it to work, and a dream alone is not proof. The answer usually comes as ease in one direction and difficulty in the other, or as a settled heart. Repeat the prayer for up to seven nights if the matter is still unclear.

Best times

Any time except the forbidden times for prayer (sunrise, sunset and midday). The last third of the night is a blessed time for dua.

Do I have to see a dream after Istikhara?

No. Guidance usually comes through ease, circumstances and a settled heart, not necessarily a dream.

Can I do Istikhara for someone else?

You can pray for them, but the decision and the prayer are best made by the person concerned.
