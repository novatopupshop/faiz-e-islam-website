Naat Lyrics

Naat lyrics to read and sing in praise of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

Tajdar-e-Haram

Tajdar-e-Haram, ho nigah-e-karam, hum ghareebon ke din bhi sanwar jayenge.
Hami-e-bekasan, kya kahega jahan, aapke dar se khali agar jayenge.

The full lyrics of Tajdar-e-Haram are among the most loved naats in South Asia — a plea to the King of the Haram for a merciful glance, and a hope that the days of the poor will be set right by his grace.

Tu Kuja Man Kuja

Tu kuja man kuja, tu kahan main kahan — you are where, and I am where. A humble naat about the distance between the servant and the beloved Prophet ﷺ, and the longing to close it.

Hasbi Rabbi Jallallah

Hasbi Rabbi Jallallah, ma fi qalbi ghayrullah — my Lord suffices me, glory be to Allah, there is nothing in my heart but Allah. A simple, well-known nasheed of reliance on Allah.

Balaghal Ula Bi Kamalihi

Balaghal ula bi kamalihi, kashafad duja bi jamalihi, hasunat jami'u khisalihi, sallu alayhi wa aalihi. He reached the highest peak by his perfection, he removed the darkness by his beauty, all his qualities are beautiful — send blessings upon him and his family.

Maula Ya Salli Wa Sallim

Maula ya salli wa sallim da'iman abadan, ala habibika khayril khalqi kullihimi. O Master, send blessings and peace always and forever upon Your beloved, the best of all creation.

Lyrics are shared for reading and learning. Audio and video versions are available from many reciters; search for the naat title on your usual audio platform.
