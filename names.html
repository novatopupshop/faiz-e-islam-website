99 Names of Allah — Asma-ul-Husna

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah has ninety-nine names; whoever memorises them will enter Paradise." (Sahih al-Bukhari). Below are all 99 names with Arabic, transliteration and meaning.

اللهُ

The God — the only one worthy of worship

الرَّحْمَنُ

الرَّحِيمُ
